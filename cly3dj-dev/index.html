Microphone

Through the mixer: turn Include mic audio OFF in Quest Camera > Settings, or your voice is recorded twice. Second option: the app leaves the mic alone and MIC only ducks the music; the recorder or BeamXR records the voice. Only one app can use the Quest mic at a time.
Feedback guard (echo cancelling; only for the headset speakers, leave off with headphones)
Press MIC on the mixer; it shows how late you hear yourself.

Mouse

Knobsdrag up/down, wheel, double-click resets (Shift = fine)
Fadersdrag
Recorddrag to scratch, click the grooves to drop the needle there
Tonearmclick: lift and park, or cue to the start
Cratewheel to flip through, click a row or sleeve, click again to pull
Held recordclick a deck to put it on, click the crate to put it back
Arrangedrag a turntable or the mixer by its sides, the crate by its walls, or a flight case by its body; wheel while dragging rotates, Shift+wheel raises or lowers gear. Gear stays where you put it and snaps onto a case only when let go just above it
Case sizedrag a flight case's bottom corner or bottom edge; Shift-drag up/down changes its height
Empty spacedrag to look around, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom

VR / passthrough

Pinch / trigger / gripnear a knob, fader, tonearm or record edge grabs it; twist to turn knobs
Fingertippress buttons and the crate screen; rest on the vinyl to hold it, move to scratch
Cratepinch inside and slide to flip through, lift out to pull the record; let go over a platter to play the side facing up
Arrangegrab gear by its sides, or a flight case by its body, and carry it; twist your hand or use the thumbstick to rotate. Gear stays exactly where you let go (no gravity); let go just above a case and it sits on it and travels with it
Recordslet go of a record anywhere and it falls or flies with your hand's momentum; drop it on an empty platter to play it, on the crate to file it; pick it up again from anywhere
Powertwist a turntable's power dial (front left) to switch it off: lights go out and the platter winds down
Case sizegrab a flight case with both hands on opposite sides: pull apart or push together to change width (left/right sides) or depth (front/back); raise or lower both hands to change height
