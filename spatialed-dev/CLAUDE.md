7. (6 Oct 2026) Phase 1 core: Spatial Vinyl lesson player. Backup: `backups/pre-007-lesson/` (main.js, index.html, sw.js).
   - New `web/js/lesson.js` (LessonPlayer). A track carries `lesson` (URL of its side.json). Each frame `lesson.step()` gets both decks' { track, heard playhead (heardPos), rate, gain }; the deck with a lesson drives it (both: the louder channel, deckGains). Everything is a pure function of t: actor clips set by `action.time = t - t0` (clamped, or wrapped with loop) and `mixer.update(0)`; trajectories revealed by draw range over [t0, t1]; a chapter board (canvas) shows the chapter at t. So pause, scratch backwards and needle drops need no special code.
   - side.json v1 format documented at the top of lesson.js (title, stage, chapters, actors, trajectories). Stage sits in rig space (moves with GEAR HERE), default [0, 0, -2.4], with a 1.7 m dark plinth.
   - Demo: `web/examples/Demo Lesson.side.json` + `demo_lesson_actor.glb` (78 KB, box figure on pivots, no skinning, clip "Demo" 65.5 s = audio - 1.0 s: look around / walk a 2 m square with a cyan floor trail drawn over 23.5 to 43.5 s / arms up). Built by `tools/make_demo_lesson_actor.py`.
   - main.js hooks: import; `lesson` + `lessonStep()` after the Stage; `lessonStep()` in the frame loop after vvStep; EXAMPLES `lesson` field passed onto side A's track; camera preset `lesson` (key 4, HUD button "Lesson") at rig [0, 2.3, 2.6] looking at [0, 0.9, -2.4].
   - The studio back wall (z -0.6) hid the stage, so it and the neon's wall glow are hidden while a lesson is showing (`lessonOn` in stepWallGlow). The neon sign itself still stands in the line of sight: owner to decide.
   - sw.js caches lesson.js. Tested here: app boots clean (only the expected XML / api/taps 404s); Demo Lesson on deck A playing, chapter board 1 -> 2 -> 3, walk and trail and arms in step at 8, 28, 37, 52 s. Not yet tested on the PC with real scratching or on the Quest.
8. (6 Oct 2026) Owner idea: the lesson starts as a diorama on the turntable and is placed in the room by hand. Backup: `backups/pre-008-diorama/` (main.js, xr.js, lesson.js, index.html).
   - lesson.js: placement on top of #7. On a deck: centred on the record, 7 cm above it (DECK_LIFT), 13 cm radius (DECK_R, scale = 0.13 / plinth radius, 0.076 for the demo), turning with the record (owner's choice). A record going on always starts on its deck (owner); its pose is free once taken off by hand.
   - Grab (xr.js `lessonGrab`): grip (bare hands: pinch) inside the volume, an invisible cylinder round the plinth up to 2.7 m local height, plus 3 cm. Volume begins 6 cm above the record, above the record's own 5 cm grab zones, so lift / scratch / spindle are untouched. Diorama-sized (scale < 0.5) it is checked before the gear; room-sized it is checked last, so gear keeps the grip.
   - One hand carries it; thumbstick up / down scales about the hand (x4 per second), left / right turns it (owner: one-hand fallback). A second grip = both hands: size follows the hand distance, the line between them turns it about vertical (never tilts), the midpoint carries it. One of two letting go = the other carries on alone.
   - Let go within 20 % of 1:1 = life size, floor on the rig floor (toast "Lesson at life size, on the floor"); let go small (< 0.35) within 20 cm of a record and up to 45 cm above it = back on that deck. Scale limits 0.02 to 1.5.
   - Outline (cyan cylinder, green when a let-go would snap to 1:1) while a hand is inside or holding.
   - Studio wall now hides only while the lesson is placed in the room (`lesson.isFree()`), not while it sits on a deck.
   - Desktop keys: G = deck <-> life size at side.json's stage position; - / = = smaller / bigger. Help table rows added (keys, and "Lesson diorama" under headset).
   - Tested here (headless, API-driven, no real controllers): on-deck pose and turning, hitTest 2 cm above record = none / 9 cm = diorama, one-hand carry, stick scale 0.076 -> 0.31 in 1 s, two-hand stretch to 0.93 -> snap 1:1 on the floor, G back to the deck. Not yet tried with Quest controllers.
