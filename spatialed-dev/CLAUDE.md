7. (6 Oct 2026) Phase 1 core: Spatial Vinyl lesson player. Backup: `backups/pre-007-lesson/` (main.js, index.html, sw.js).
   - New `web/js/lesson.js` (LessonPlayer). A track carries `lesson` (URL of its side.json). Each frame `lesson.step()` gets both decks' { track, heard playhead (heardPos), rate, gain }; the deck with a lesson drives it (both: the louder channel, deckGains). Everything is a pure function of t: actor clips set by `action.time = t - t0` (clamped, or wrapped with loop) and `mixer.update(0)`; trajectories revealed by draw range over [t0, t1]; a chapter board (canvas) shows the chapter at t. So pause, scratch backwards and needle drops need no special code.
   - side.json v1 format documented at the top of lesson.js (title, stage, chapters, actors, trajectories). Stage sits in rig space (moves with GEAR HERE), default [0, 0, -2.4], with a 1.7 m dark plinth.

12. (6 Oct 2026) Owner: with the record stopped or held, Max's neck kept twisting (and after the walk, arms and legs kept swinging); and the dollhouse should not turn with the record. Backup: `backups/pre-012-freeze/lesson.js`.
   - Cause: three's AnimationMixer (PropertyMixer.apply) writes a bone only when its clip value changed. With t standing still the actor's own idle clip wrote nothing, and #9's borrowed turn (bone.quaternion.multiply) was added again every frame on top of last frame's result. Fix in applyMotion: per mapped bone keep `base` (its own clip pose) and `written` (what we set last frame); if the bone still equals `written`, the mixer left it, so put `base` back before adding the turn. Bones the idle never animates keep their rest pose as base.
   - Tested: same t drawn 200 times = no change (was up to 1.56 in quaternion units); scrub 30 -> 50 -> 12 -> 30 gives the identical pose; full app with the deck stopped: head identical over 4 s, t frozen at 10.087.
   - On the deck the diorama now takes its turn from the deck group (record's parent), not the spinning record (owner, reverses the #8 choice). Playing: lesson yaw stays 0 while the record angle changes. Students follow (packet y).
