Part 1: Hosting Your Site for Free

Your Moondance Estate site is a static website – just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. That means it works perfectly on any of these 100% free platforms. All of them give you a live URL to share immediately.

Platform Best for Key Free Features
GitHub Pages Simple, one‑page sites Free custom domain, HTTPS, unlimited traffic
Netlify Drag‑and‑drop + Git workflows Free SSL, global CDN, form handling, 100 GB bandwidth/month
Vercel Modern frontend projects Unlimited deployments, custom domains, HTTPS, 100 GB bandwidth/month
Cloudflare Pages Performance + security Unlimited bandwidth, global CDN, built‑in analytics

All four are free forever – no credit card required.

Option A: Netlify (Easiest – Drag & Drop)

  1. Go to Netlify.com and sign up for a free account.
  2. On your dashboard, drag your index.html file (and any assets like images) into the box that says “Drag and drop your site folder here”.
  3. That’s it – Netlify will give you a live URL in a few seconds.
  4. Optional: Connect your GitHub repository – any time you push changes, Netlify will auto‑redeploy.

Option B: GitHub Pages (Best for Version Control)

  1. Create a free account at GitHub.com.
  2. Create a new repository (e.g., moondance-estate).
  3. Upload your index.html file to that repository.
  4. Go to Settings → Pages and under “Branch” select main (or master).
  5. Save – your site will be live at https://your-username.github.io/moondance-estate/.

Option C: Vercel (One‑Click from GitHub)

  1. Sign up at Vercel.com.
  2. Click “Add New…” → “Project” and import your GitHub repository.
  3. Vercel will automatically detect your HTML file and deploy it.

Part 2: Customising Your Site

All content lives in index.html. Open it in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code, Sublime Text, etc.) and use the guides below to make changes.

🔄 Changing Background Videos

Each section’s background video is defined inside a <video> tag. Currently they all use one working URL. To replace any video:

  1. Find the <video> tag for the section you want to change.
  2. Locate the <source src="..."> line inside it.
  3. Replace the URL with your own direct video link (must end in .mp4).

Example – Hero video:

<video class="hero-video" autoplay muted loop playsinline preload="auto" ...> <source src="https://cdn.pixabay.com/video/2023/11/28/191159-889246512_large.mp4" type="video/mp4" /> ... </video>
💡 How to get your own video URL:
Upload your video to a free service like Cloudinary, ImgBB, or Google Drive (set sharing to “Anyone with the link”). Copy the direct .mp4 link and paste it into src="".
✅ Important: Videos must be in MP4 format and ideally in 16:9 landscape to fit the design properly.

💬 Updating Testimonials

Testimonials live inside the <div class="testimonial-grid"> block. Each one follows this structure:

<div class="testimonial-card"> <div class="stars">★★★★★</div> <blockquote>“Your testimonial text goes here.”</blockquote> <div class="client"> <div class="avatar"><img src="avatar-image-url.jpg" alt="Name" /></div> <div> <div class="name">Guest Name</div> <div class="role">Cottage Name, Month Year</div> </div> </div> </div>

To edit:

  • Change the text inside <blockquote>.
  • Update the guest’s name and role.
  • Replace the avatar image URL with your own photo link.

You can add or remove as many testimonials as you like – just copy/paste the entire <div class="testimonial-card"> block.

📞 Changing Contact Information

Find the <div class="contact-details"> section:

<div class="detail"><span class="icon">📍</span><span>Moondance Estate, Nilgiris, TN</span></div> <div class="detail"><span class="icon">📞</span><span><a href="tel:+919876543210">+91 98765 43210</a></span></div> <div class="detail"><span class="icon">✉️</span><span><a href="mailto:hello@moondanceestate.in">hello@moondanceestate.in</a></span></div>

To update:

  • Change the address text.
  • Replace the phone number in both the href="tel:..." and the visible text.
  • Replace the email in both the href="mailto:..." and the visible text.

Social / Instagram link – located just below:

<div class="detail" ...> <span class="icon">📸</span> <span><a href="https://www.instagram.com/moondanceestate/reel/DZFJ1G0Jgbb/" target="_blank" style="color:#d4a373; text-decoration:underline;">Watch our reel on Instagram</a></span> </div>

Replace the URL with your own Instagram (or any other social) link.
